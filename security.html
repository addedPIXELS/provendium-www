Privacy at the foundation.

Provendium® holds a Province's record and its members' details, so it is built for the discretion Freemasonry expects. Privacy is designed in from the start, not added on afterwards.

How your data is protected

Members trust their Province with their information. Looking after it carefully has been part of Provendium from the first day.

The data belongs to the Province
Provendium looks after it on the Province's behalf. The Province is the data controller and decides how its data is used.
Each Province kept separate
One Province's information is never visible to another. Separation is enforced in three layers of the system, so if one ever missed something the other two would still hold.
Sensitive details encrypted
Email addresses, telephone numbers and dates of birth are encrypted with a key unique to each Province. Dietary and health information is encrypted too.
Members see only what they should
What a person can see and do follows the role they hold. Area leaders see their own Areas, and a Secretary sees their own Lodge.
Sign in without a password
Members sign in with a secure link sent to them, or with a passkey using Face ID or Touch ID, so there is no password to forget or reuse.
The same rules for the app and the API
The Provendium app and any connected website follow the same permissions. Feeds are limited to the websites a Province approves, and their keys can be changed at any time.
Backed up and recoverable
A backup is taken before every import and every release. Recovery is practised, not assumed.

Careful with every change

Daily updates to the record are checked before they are applied, and a file that looks wrong is held back. Changes to the platform are released to a separate staging system and checked there first. Our own testing runs on anonymised data, never on real contact details.

Yours to question

Provendium is an independent platform, run by addedPIXELS. We are glad to talk through data protection with whoever holds that responsibility in your Province, and to answer questions before anything is committed.

Common questions about data

Who can see our Province's information?

Only people from your Province, and only what their role allows. No other Province can see your data, and a member sees the public diary plus whatever concerns them directly, not the whole administration.

Where is the data held?

Provendium is hosted in the UK and EU, with each Province's records isolated from every other. We are happy to set out the hosting arrangements in detail for a Province considering it.

Can other websites or services get at our data through the API?

Only the ones your Province approves, and only for what they need. A Lodge website can show its own calendar. It cannot read members' contact details. The Province can withdraw a connection at any time.

Do you use real member data to test the system?

No. Our development and testing environments run on anonymised data, with placeholder contact details rather than the real thing.

What happens to our data if we stop using Provendium?

It remains your Province's data. We will return it and remove it on request, and we will put the arrangements in writing.

Have a question about data protection?

Ask us anything before you decide. We would rather answer it now than have you wonder.
