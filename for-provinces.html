Getting started, step by step

  1. Get in touch

    Tell us about your Province and what you would like Provendium to do. We will talk it through and answer your questions, including on data protection.

  2. We bring your records across

    Your existing membership records are imported and checked: Brethren, units, offices, ranks and the forward calendar. You start from where you already are.

  3. Set up with your Office

    We work with the Provincial Office to get the diary, the officers and the settings right, and to make sure the people who run it recognise their own Province in it.

  4. Publish your calendar

    Put the calendar on your Provincial website and open the feeds, so members can see and follow the diary straight away.

  5. Switch on the rest when you are ready

    Invitations, dining and seating, ticketing, the Summons Builder, reporting and the app each come in at the pace that suits you. Each Lodge and Chapter decides whether to use the Lodge tools.

What Provinces ask us

Does it cover both Craft and Royal Arch?

Yes. The diary, the registers and the meetings handle Craft and Royal Arch together, and a Brother who serves in both appears as one person with both affiliations.

Do we have to take everything at once?

No. Most Provinces start with the diary and the website calendar, then bring in the other parts as they are ready. You set the pace.

Does it replace the records Grand Lodge holds?

No. The official Grand Lodge records remain the official records. Provendium works from the membership records your Province already keeps and holds the Province's own working information around them.

Will it fit our existing website?

The calendar is styled to sit within your Provincial website and is limited to your own domains. It appears on your page rather than sending members elsewhere.

Do our Lodges have to change how they work?

No. The Lodge and Chapter tools are there for the units that want them. Nothing changes how a unit runs unless it chooses to take them up.
