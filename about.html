Built for the way Provinces work.

A Province's year takes a lot of organising. Provendium® is meant to make that work simpler and safer, without changing the traditions it serves.

The problem we set out to solve

Most Provinces run on a patchwork. The diary sits in one place, officer lists in another, invitations go out by email, and a good deal is held only in the Secretary's head.

Each of those tools serves a purpose. But where the same information is kept in several places, it takes extra effort to keep up to date and to protect. It works until something is missed, or someone moves on.

The diary was the most urgent part of that, so it was the first thing Provendium solved. It was never the whole plan.

A compendium, not a calendar

Provincial compendium

A compendium gathers everything of value into one authoritative volume. Provendium is designed as a single source of truth for Provincial information: one trusted record, kept current every day, underneath every service.

The diary, the Summons Builder, ticketing, dining, reporting and the member app are all views onto that one record. New parts are added on the same foundation rather than as separate systems with their own copies of members' details. That is why they agree with each other, and why a new one can be switched on without starting again.
